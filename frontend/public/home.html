Technical Specifications

What are the standard drilling tolerances?
Standard mechanical drilling tolerance is ±0.075mm. For laser drilling (microvias), tolerances can be held to ±0.05mm. Aspect ratios up to 10:1 are supported on standard 1.6mm boards.
Do you support blind and buried vias?
Yes, for 4+ layer boards. Blind vias connect an outer layer to one or more inner layers. Buried vias connect two or more inner layers. Please specify via stackup in your Gerber documentation.
Is V-Scoring available for panelization?
Yes. V-scoring requires a minimum board thickness of 0.6mm. We recommend a 1/3 cut depth on both top and bottom. Ensure 0.5mm clearance from copper to the V-cut line.
